* Recognition & Highlights

→ A selection of productions, releases and projects that represent my work across theatre, music and design.

✦

Chudacźek

Composed the original music for Chudacźek, a theatre production written and performed by Katarína Hudačková. The work extended beyond composition to live audio engineering and the creation of venue-specific sound designs, adapting the production's sonic environment to each performance space. The production was presented at multiple festivals, including Kremnické Gagy 2025, and received press coverage through an interview with Katarína Hudačková published by Denník N.

✦

Som Šašo a som len na smiech?

A graduation project written and designed around the paradox of the sad clown. The book explores the history of theatre and clowning, from traditional theatrical practices to contemporary initiatives such as Red Noses International. Developed in two formats: a square edition and a half-A5 edition.

✦

Adults Are Definitely Very Weird

My original theatrical score was composed for Adults Are Definitely Very Weird, a stage production that received second place at the regional round of the Vajanského Martin theatre competition.

✦

Not That Far Away

Midheaven's album Not That Far Away caught the attention of Radio Rapes, leading to an invitation for an interview and a feature discussing the album and the band's music.
