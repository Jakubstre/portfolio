A Christmas campaign concept for Upratovanie Liptov, built around the idea that not everything has to be done personally. The campaign positions professional cleaning as a way to save time and enjoy the holidays without the stress of household preparations. The creative concept translates familiar Christmas activities into a series of humorous social media visuals, supported by a broader communication and advertising strategy.

* HIGHLIGHTS

→ SCOPE & IMPACT

  • Development of the campaign concept and central message.
  • Definition of target audiences and communication channels.
  • Creative direction and visual design of a seven-slide social media carousel.
  • Creation of AI-generated voxel-style Christmas illustrations.
  • Development of proposed Meta Ads, local promotion and conversion strategy.
  • Definition of campaign performance indicators and measurement framework.
