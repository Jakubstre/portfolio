→ LISTEN TO THE PROJECT

AN ORIGINAL COLLABORATIVE COMPOSITION DEVELOPED INTO AN EXTENSIVE ORCHESTRAL MUSIC PRODUCTION, FEATURING INTRICATE STRING ARRANGEMENTS, LAYERED VOCALS, ACOUSTIC INSTRUMENTATION, AND INDEPENDENT RECORDING, MIXING AND MASTERING.

* HIGHLIGHTS

→ SCOPE & IMPACT

  • Developed the most instrumentally extensive and orchestral music production in the composer's portfolio to date
  • Co-written and composed with five additional contributors
  • Created extensive string arrangements covering legato, pizzicato, staccato and tremolo techniques
  • Recorded and arranged multiple orchestral layers, including violins, violas, cellos and double bass
  • Performed and recorded a wide range of instruments, including piano, acoustic guitar, combo organ and clarinet
  • Recorded lead, harmony and background vocal arrangements alongside additional vocal contributors
  • Managed recording, additional engineering, production, mixing and mastering
  • Developed the complete visual identity of the physical release, including CD artwork, booklet, label and packaging
