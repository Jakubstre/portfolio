→ Theatre Compositions

Get to know more about all of my Theatre Compositions!

Since 2022, I have composed original music for theatre productions, combining songwriting, recording, production and sound engineering. I have composed music for three theatre productions, one of which was performed at multiple festivals across Slovakia and the Czech Republic, while another was awarded third place at the national round of the 72nd Hviezdoslavov Kubín competition.

→ Collaboration

Looking for an original musical voice for your next performance?

I compose original music for theatre, dance, pantomime and multidisciplinary performances. Every project begins with the story and grows through close collaboration with the creative team. If my work resonates with you, I'd be glad to hear about your next production.
